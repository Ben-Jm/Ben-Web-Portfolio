Banking Web app End-to-end

Stanbic Bank Accelerate web app.

Customer-facing banking web application — research through engineering handoff.

Context

Banking complexity, customer-facing simplicity.

A customer-facing banking journey where the underlying product was complex but the user needed to move through it confidently. The work was full-lifecycle — research, IA, prototyping, usability testing, and engineering partnership through delivery.

Application journey screens and the post-confirmation account view.

What I did

Full lifecycle, partnered with engineering through delivery.

I led user research, information architecture, wireframing, prototyping, and usability testing. I partnered with engineering through delivery to keep the shipped product aligned with the validated design — through UAT, design QA, and release sign-off.
