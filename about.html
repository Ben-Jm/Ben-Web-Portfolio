Info

User-focused designer with five years of experience translating user insights into intuitive flows and interfaces that improve UX and move business metrics.

I work across the full design lifecycle — user research and behavioural data, journey mapping, prototyping, usability testing, accessibility, design systems, and engineering handover into production. I've shipped products in telecommunications, financial services, mining, fintech, and public-sector self-service. The sectors change; the discipline doesn't.

I run continuous-improvement cycles using analytics (Microsoft Clarity, GA4) and treat shipped work as the start of the loop, not the end of it. Comfortable leading design across research, engineering, marketing, and commercial teams to take products from validated prototype into production.

Currently Digital Systems Experience Lead at Spectrum Analytics in Gaborone.

How I work

Four steps, in order

From research to released

Listen

Start with research, interviews, and the data already in the building. The full picture before the proposal.

Design

Shape interfaces around real user needs, not assumptions. Test screens and flows with people.

Apply technology with intent

Pick what fits the problem — sometimes a polished UI, sometimes automation, sometimes a process change.

Ship and iterate

Deliver, partner through release, then measure and improve once it's live.
